## what is this

You put on a headset. Your room is still there. Floating in it: a wall of glass,
and a world in the middle of it that you can grab with your hands.

Pinch it. Spin it. Pull it apart. Ask it questions.

Then find the slider hidden in the settings, drag it right, and watch your room
dissolve into a starfield while the world keeps turning.

That's it. That's the app.

---

## ✋ how to touch it

| you do | it does |
|---|---|
| **pinch** | select |
| **poke** | press |
| **pinch + drag** | orbit the world |
| **two hands, pull apart** | resize the world |
| **the hidden slider** | delete your room |

Controllers work too, if you're into that.

## 🌍 four worlds

**Coastal City** — a living urban twin. Traffic actually flows. Turbines actually
spin at the actual wind speed outside your actual window.

**Mars Analogue** — orbiters on real tracks, a terminator that moves, landing
sites ranked by an actual score.

**Cloud Forest** — waterfall, mist, pollen, and four species that care whether
the canopy holds.

**Turbine XJ-9** — pull it apart along its axis and watch the heat map bloom.

---

## 🔮 the trick

Nothing is downloaded. No meshes. No textures. No 400 MB asset bundle.

Every building, every tree, every star is **generated in code at runtime**, which
means it's infinitely sharp no matter how far you pinch it open — and the whole
thing ships in about the size of a JPEG.

The bloom is fake. The glass is fake. It looks better than the real thing.

---

## 🌦 it knows what the weather is

Real feeds, live, no API keys:

- **Open-Meteo** — temperature, wind, cloud, solar irradiance, air quality
- **USGS** — every earthquake on Earth in the last 24 hours

The wind outside drives the turbines. The sun drives the panels. The rain pushes
commuters into their cars and the traffic model notices.

Everything the model infers is labelled `MODEL`. Nothing pretends to be
measured when it isn't.

---

## 🧠 the copilot

Ask it things. It answers from the numbers actually on screen, computes the
answer, and sometimes reaches over and moves a slider for you.

It has never hallucinated a figure, because it can't. It doesn't know how to
make things up — it only knows how to do arithmetic on what's in front of it.

<sub>(bring your own OpenRouter key in Settings if you want it to get chattier)</sub>
